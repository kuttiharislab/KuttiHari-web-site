How this website handles your information

Preview version · 7 October 2026

Booking enquiries

The booking form on this preview does not send information to, or store information on, this web server. When you submit it, your device opens your email application with the details placed into a draft addressed to kuttiharislab@gmail.com. You decide whether to send that email.

Information you may provide

A booking email may include your name, email address, event type, proposed date, location and the details you choose to write. This information is used to understand and respond to your enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This preview does not use analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies.

External services

Links to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube take you to those providers, whose own privacy terms apply. Embedded third-party tracking is not used on this preview.

Retention, recipients and your rights

The final production notice must be completed after the business confirms who receives enquiries, how long correspondence is retained, which service providers process it, the applicable lawful basis and the controller’s full identity. Until then, this preview is intentionally marked noindex and should not replace the production site.

Contact

For privacy questions, email kuttiharislab@gmail.com.

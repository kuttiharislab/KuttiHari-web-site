01 Kutti story

Europe’s first Tamil stand-up comedian

Comedy that feels close to home — wherever home is.

Kutti Hari is a London-based Tamil stand-up comedian known for quick wit, crowd connection and high-energy performances. He has shared stages with leading figures from Tamil film and independent entertainment, including Akshara Haasan, Dhanush and Kala Master.

Off the comedy stage, he hosts corporate events, cultural shows, weddings and private celebrations with a balance of humour, structure and heart.
